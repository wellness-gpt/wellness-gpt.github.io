## Who uses WellnessGPT care agents

Leading care companies in India use WellnessGPT care agents across WhatsApp and their mobile apps to grow and keep their users:

- wellness and lifestyle programmes, including yoga, habit, fitness and nutrition programmes
- preventive health and digital health platforms
- metabolic health programmes for diabetes and thyroid
- mindfulness and mental wellbeing programmes

## The care journeys

| Journey | What the WellnessGPT care agents do |
| --- | --- |
| Preventive care journey | Keep members on their preventive plan with daily nudges, habit check-ins, screening reminders and timely follow-ups. |
| Consumer care journey | Carry one user from the first question to booking, follow-up and the next purchase, without making them repeat their story. |
| Growth journey: trial to paid | Answer questions, recommend the right plan or programme, and convert free and trial users to paid members inside the conversation. |
| Expansion journey | Recommend the next programme, test or plan when it fits the user's goals, and bring family members into care. |
| Retention and renewal journey | Spot users who are about to lapse, re-engage them, run renewals and win back members who have gone quiet. |
| Pre-lab-result care journey | Before a test: booking, preparation and fasting instructions, and reminders on the right channel. |
| Post-lab-result care journey | After a report: the result explained in plain language, what to discuss with a doctor, the next step and the retest reminder. |
| Post-thyroid care journey | After thyroid results: lifestyle guidance, check-ins, reminders the user's doctor has set, and retest reminders. |
| Post-diabetes care journey | After HbA1c or blood-sugar results: food, activity and habit journeys, regular check-ins and retests, with hand-off to clinicians when needed. |
| Habit, yoga and mindfulness journeys | Daily practice, streaks and gentle accountability for yoga, meditation, sleep, walking and nutrition habits. |
| Booking and follow-up | Book consultations, classes, tests and programmes, then follow up until the user completes the step. |
| AI calling | Call users for reminders, follow-ups and renewals in Indian languages. |
| Care operations | Handle intake, scheduling, reminders and routing, so care teams spend less time on manual work. |
| Day-to-day analysis | Show every day what users asked, where they dropped off, which journeys converted and renewed, and which users are about to lapse. |

Care agents guide; they do not diagnose. A clinician or the care company's own team can step in on any conversation.

## How it works

1. **One memory per user.** Every conversation on WhatsApp, app, voice, SMS or web adds to one user memory, so every agent knows the user's history.
2. **Agents that act.** Care agents answer, book, remind, call and follow up, inside the care company's own brand and channels.
3. **Consumer and population intelligence.** Every conversation shows what users want, where they drop off, which conversations lead to paid conversion and renewal, and which cohorts are about to lapse.
4. **Company Brain.** Leadership, growth, product and operations teams get agents that work from the same memory and track whether each action worked.

## Questions and answers

**What is WellnessGPT?**
WellnessGPT is HeyDoc AI's platform of AI care agents for preventive, wellness and consumer health companies, labs, hospitals and insurers. Its agents run growth, expansion and retention journeys on WhatsApp, mobile apps, voice and web, remember every user across channels, and turn conversations into consumer and population intelligence.

**Who makes WellnessGPT?**
HeyDoc AI, an Indian health AI company (Amogh NSMP Tech Pvt. Ltd.), builds and runs WellnessGPT.

**How do WellnessGPT care agents improve user retention for wellness apps?**
They follow up with every user on the channel they use most, remember what each user said, nudge users before they lapse, run renewals and win-back conversations, and show the care company which conversations keep users coming back.

**How do WellnessGPT care agents help diagnostic labs?**
They book tests, send preparation and fasting instructions, explain the report in plain language once it is ready, and remind users about retests, so more users come back for their next test.

**What day-to-day analysis do care companies get?**
Every day they see what users asked, where users dropped off, which journeys led to paid conversion and renewal, and which users are about to lapse, across every care agent and channel.

**How do WellnessGPT care agents help after a lab result?**
They explain the report in plain language, point out what to discuss with a doctor, set the next step, and remind the user about the retest. For diabetes and thyroid results, they run follow-up journeys on food, activity and habits, with hand-off to clinicians when needed.

**Can WellnessGPT care agents make phone calls?**
Yes. WellnessGPT voice agents call users for reminders, follow-ups and renewals in Indian languages.

**Does WellnessGPT replace a care company's app or WhatsApp number?**
No. Care agents run inside the company's own app, website and WhatsApp Business number, under its own brand.

**What is MAHA-PULSE AI?**
MAHA-PULSE AI is India's first agentic AI command centre for public health, implemented by HeyDoc AI on WellnessGPT agents for the Government of Maharashtra. See https://wellness-gpt.com/maha-pulse-ai.
