MAHA-PULSE AI: India's first agentic AI command centre for public health

Maharashtra's Health Minister Prakash Abitkar launched MAHA-PULSE AI in Kolhapur on 16 November 2026. HeyDoc AI implements it for the Public Health Department on WellnessGPT agents.

It shows officials doctor presence, crowding, cleanliness and emergencies as they happen, and gives every citizen a 24x7 WellnessGPT care companion in Marathi, Hindi and English.
