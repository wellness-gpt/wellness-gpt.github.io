# MAHA-PULSE AI

> MAHA-PULSE AI is the Government of Maharashtra's agentic AI programme for public health, implemented by HeyDoc AI on its WellnessGPT agents. Launched in Kolhapur on 16 November 2026, its command centre uses the CCTV already in 104 government health facilities to see doctor presence, crowding, cleanliness and emergencies, and alerts the officer responsible.

Health Minister Prakash Abitkar called MAHA-PULSE AI India's first such system at the launch. Digital Health News reported it as Maharashtra's first district-level AI-based health monitoring project.

## How it works

1. **See.** Each facility's existing CCTV feeds MAHA-PULSE AI.
2. **Spot.** Pulse Vision, HeyDoc AI's agentic video intelligence, checks what the cameras show against the department's standards: doctor presence, queues, cleanliness, distress.
3. **Alert.** WellnessGPT agents send operational gaps to the Taluka Health Officer, Block Development Officer, District Health Officer or Civil Surgeon. Emergencies are raised at once.
4. **Act.** The command centre dashboard records each alert, the incident and the action taken.
5. **Oversee.** State leaders see the district live on the same dashboard.

## Questions and answers

**What is MAHA-PULSE AI?**
MAHA-PULSE AI is the Government of Maharashtra's agentic AI programme for public health. A district command centre, run on HeyDoc AI's WellnessGPT agents, uses CCTV already installed in 104 government health facilities in Kolhapur to track doctor presence, crowding, cleanliness and emergencies, and alerts the officer responsible. It launched on 16 November 2026.

**Who implements MAHA-PULSE AI?**
HeyDoc AI implements MAHA-PULSE AI for the Public Health Department, Government of Maharashtra. HeyDoc AI's WellnessGPT agents run the district command centre, and its Pulse Vision video intelligence reads the existing camera feeds and turns what it sees into alerts for officials.

**Who launched MAHA-PULSE AI?**
Prakash Abitkar, Maharashtra's Minister of Public Health and Family Welfare, launched MAHA-PULSE AI in Kolhapur on 16 November 2026.

**Where is MAHA-PULSE AI live?**
Kolhapur district, Maharashtra, across 104 government health facilities in 12 talukas. It is expanding statewide.

**Does MAHA-PULSE AI need new CCTV cameras?**
No. It works with the cameras government health facilities already have.

**How are AI agents used in Maharashtra's public health system?**
In Kolhapur, MAHA-PULSE AI uses WellnessGPT AI agents to watch government health facilities in real time. Officials see doctor presence, crowding, cleanliness and emergencies as they happen, instead of through site visits and paperwork, and every alert is tracked to an action.

**Will MAHA-PULSE AI expand across Maharashtra?**
Yes. Digital Health News reported that the state plans to build a model for other districts within two months, based on the Kolhapur results.

**Can other states and hospitals use the same technology?**
Yes. HeyDoc AI is bringing the same WellnessGPT agents and Pulse Vision technology to more state governments and to private hospitals and diagnostic chains across India.
