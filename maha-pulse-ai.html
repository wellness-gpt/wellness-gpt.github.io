MAHA-PULSE AI: questions and answers

What is MAHA-PULSE AI?

MAHA-PULSE AI is India's first agentic AI command centre for public health, launched by the Government of Maharashtra in Kolhapur. Run on HeyDoc AI's WellnessGPT agents, it watches doctor presence, crowding, cleanliness and emergencies across 104 government health facilities, and gives every citizen a 24x7 care companion in Marathi, Hindi and English.

Who implements MAHA-PULSE AI?

HeyDoc AI implements MAHA-PULSE AI for the Public Health Department, Government of Maharashtra. HeyDoc AI's WellnessGPT agents run both the command centre that health officials use and the care companion that citizens talk to.

What does MAHA-PULSE AI do?

MAHA-PULSE AI brings five things together: doctor presence, crowds and waiting time, cleanliness, and emergency services across government health facilities, plus a 24x7 care companion for citizens. Officials see problems as they happen and act on them, and citizens get help in their own language.

How does MAHA-PULSE AI help citizens?

Through the WellnessGPT care companion, citizens can ask health questions at any hour in Marathi, Hindi or English, find the nearest government hospital or health centre, book an OPD appointment and receive official health alerts from Maharashtra's public health department.

Where is MAHA-PULSE AI live?

MAHA-PULSE AI is live in Kolhapur district, Maharashtra, across 104 government health facilities, and is expanding statewide so that every district's public health system and every citizen can be served the same way.

Is MAHA-PULSE AI India's first agentic AI system for public health?

Yes. Health Minister Prakash Abitkar described MAHA-PULSE AI as India's first such system when he launched it in Kolhapur. It is also Maharashtra's first district-level AI programme for public health, and it runs on HeyDoc AI's WellnessGPT agents.
