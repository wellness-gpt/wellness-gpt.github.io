MAHA-PULSE AI: India's first agentic AI command centre for public health

Talk with our team Read the launch coverage

MAHA-PULSE AI is the Government of Maharashtra's agentic AI programme for public health, implemented by HeyDoc AI on its WellnessGPT agents. Launched in Kolhapur on 16 November 2026, its command centre uses the CCTV already in 104 government health facilities to see doctor presence, crowding, cleanliness and emergencies, and alerts the officer responsible.

Health Minister Prakash Abitkar called it India's first such system at the launch. Digital Health News reported it as Maharashtra's first district-level AI health monitoring project. Source: Digital Health News

What the WellnessGPT agents watch in every facility

The agents look at what the department cares about most, every hour the facility is open.

Doctor presence

Whether doctors are at their OPD desks during working hours, so gaps are seen while patients are still waiting.

Crowds and waiting time

OPD congestion, how many people are waiting and for how long, so a crowded OPD is flagged before waits run into hours.

Cleanliness

Housekeeping and hygiene in wards, waiting areas and common spaces, checked through the day instead of on inspection days.

Emergencies

A patient who collapses or a fire, spotted as it happens so the authorities are alerted at once.

MAHA-PULSE AI: questions and answers

What is MAHA-PULSE AI?

MAHA-PULSE AI is the Government of Maharashtra's agentic AI programme for public health. A district command centre, run on HeyDoc AI's WellnessGPT agents, uses CCTV already installed in 104 government health facilities in Kolhapur to track doctor presence, crowding, cleanliness and emergencies, and alerts the officer responsible. It launched on 16 November 2026.

What does MAHA-PULSE stand for?

MAHA-PULSE stands for Maharashtra's Platform for Unified Lifecare Support & Engagement. The programme is also written as Maha Pulse AI or MahaPulse AI, as महा-पल्स एआय in Marathi and as महा-पल्स एआई in Hindi.

Who implements MAHA-PULSE AI?

HeyDoc AI implements MAHA-PULSE AI for the Public Health Department, Government of Maharashtra. HeyDoc AI's WellnessGPT agents run the district command centre, and its Pulse Vision video intelligence reads the existing camera feeds and turns what it sees into alerts for officials.

Who launched MAHA-PULSE AI?

Prakash Abitkar, Maharashtra's Minister of Public Health and Family Welfare, launched MAHA-PULSE AI in Kolhapur on 16 November 2026. The Public Health Department and the Kolhapur District Collectorate run it, with funding from the Kolhapur District Planning Committee's innovative initiatives fund.

Where is MAHA-PULSE AI live?

MAHA-PULSE AI is live in Kolhapur district, Maharashtra, and is expanding statewide. Its command centre connects 104 government health facilities across 12 talukas: 1 general hospital, 5 sub-district hospitals, 15 rural hospitals and 83 primary health centres.

What do the MAHA-PULSE AI agents monitor?

The WellnessGPT agents behind MAHA-PULSE AI monitor doctor attendance, patient footfall, OPD crowding, how many people are waiting and for how long, cleanliness and housekeeping, and emergencies such as a patient collapsing or a fire, so officials can act while it still matters.

Does MAHA-PULSE AI need new CCTV cameras?

No. MAHA-PULSE AI works with the CCTV cameras already installed in government health facilities. At the launch, Health Minister Prakash Abitkar said the project does not require new CCTV cameras, which keeps the cost low and lets more districts adopt it quickly.

Who receives MAHA-PULSE AI alerts?

Alerts about operational gaps go to the Taluka Health Officer, Block Development Officer, District Health Officer and Civil Surgeon. The Health Minister, the Health Department Secretary and the Commissioner can view the same command centre dashboard, with alerts, incidents and the action taken.

Is MAHA-PULSE AI India's first agentic AI system for public health?

Health Minister Prakash Abitkar called MAHA-PULSE AI India's first such system when he launched it in Kolhapur. Digital Health News reported it as Maharashtra's first district-level AI-based health monitoring project. It runs on HeyDoc AI's WellnessGPT agents.

How are AI agents used in Maharashtra's public health system?

In Kolhapur, MAHA-PULSE AI uses WellnessGPT AI agents to watch government health facilities in real time. Officials see doctor presence, crowding, cleanliness and emergencies as they happen, instead of through site visits and paperwork, and every alert is tracked to an action.

Will MAHA-PULSE AI expand across Maharashtra?

Yes. MAHA-PULSE AI starts in Kolhapur and is expanding statewide. Digital Health News reported that the state plans to build a model for other districts within two months, based on the results of the Kolhapur programme.

Can other states and hospitals use MAHA-PULSE AI technology?

Yes. HeyDoc AI is bringing the same WellnessGPT agents and Pulse Vision technology to more state governments and to private hospitals and diagnostic chains across India. Health departments and hospital networks can talk to the HeyDoc AI team about a deployment.
